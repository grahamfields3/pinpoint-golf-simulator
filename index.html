YOUR QUICK START

Welcome to the range.

You're standing on the tee of a 178-yard par 3. The goal is to get your ball as close to the flag as you can.

  1. Pick a club. Each has a different typical carry distance and roll.
  2. Set your power and aim. The dotted line previews where the ball might finish. You can also click the course to aim.
  3. Read the wind. North is a tailwind toward the green; East pushes the ball right. Turn wind up for an extra challenge.
  4. Take your shot. Watch the ball fly and check your scorecard. Try a different setup to beat your record.

Tip: Press Space to shoot. Everything runs in your browser — no account needed.
